Services

What Grid Logic Delivers

Seven service lines designed to solve real operational problems for growing businesses.

01

Workflow Automation

We build systems to handle your repetitive tasks automatically — using tools you already have.

Expect: Meaningful reduction in manual processing time — typically visible within the first month.

02

Business Process Improvement

We map your workflows, find what's slowing you down, and redesign processes so everything runs smoothly.

Expect: Faster turnaround times, fewer errors, and a team that knows exactly what to do.

03

Operational Consulting

Expert advisory to identify what to fix and build a clear, prioritized plan to do it.

Expect: A prioritized roadmap so you invest your time where it matters most.

04

Reporting & Dashboards

Custom dashboards and automated reports — many built entirely in Excel — so you always know your numbers.

Expect: Real-time visibility — no more waiting for someone to compile a report.

05

Financial Process Optimization

Streamlined invoicing, reconciliation, and financial reporting so money stops slipping through the cracks.

Expect: Faster month-end close and financial reports ready when you need them.

06

AI-Powered Productivity

Practical AI tools integrated into your workflow — to work faster, write better, and analyse smarter.

Expect: More output from the same team with less time on repetitive cognitive work.

07

Data Analysis & Business Intelligence

We turn data you already have into clear insights that drive smarter decisions across your business.

Expect: Stop guessing — know which products perform, where revenue comes from, and what needs attention.
