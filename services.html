What Grid Logic Delivers

Six service lines designed to solve real problems for small businesses — whatever industry you're in.

Workflow & Process Automation

  • Plumbers & Contractors
  • Hardware Stores
  • Car Washes

Reconciliation & Bookkeeping

We set up reconciliation tools and bookkeeping systems that automatically match transactions and flag discrepancies — so you always know exactly where your money stands.

  • Retail & Service Businesses
  • Growing Companies

  • Established Companies

Website Launches

  • Local Service Businesses

  • Retail
