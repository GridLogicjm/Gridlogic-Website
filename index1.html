Our Story

Why Grid Logic Exists

Too many Jamaican and Caribbean businesses are running on outdated processes — not because they lack ambition, but because operational transformation has historically felt out of reach. The major consulting firms are expensive, remote, and often disconnected from the regional business reality.

Grid Logic was founded to close that gap. We bring the methodologies of elite global consulting firms directly to businesses right here — combining deep knowledge of the regional market with world-class operational frameworks, intelligent technology, and a genuine commitment to client success.

The name reflects our philosophy. A grid is both structure and clarity — the foundation upon which complex systems become manageable. Logic is our method: rigorous, evidence-based, and deliberately designed to produce results. Together, Grid Logic is the trusted partner that helps businesses build the operational infrastructure they need to grow.

Our Mission

To Deliver Measurable Operational Excellence to Every Client We Serve

We exist to help businesses work smarter — eliminating waste, automating the routine, and equipping leaders with the clarity and tools to make better decisions, faster.

Our Vision

A Caribbean Business Community That Operates at World-Class Standards

We envision a region where Jamaican and Caribbean enterprises compete and win on the global stage — not despite their context, but because of the operational excellence they've built within it.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The complimentary consultation is a focused 45-minute strategy session where we explore your current operational challenges, understand your business goals, and provide initial observations and recommendations. You'll walk away with genuine insight into where your biggest efficiency opportunities lie — regardless of whether you choose to engage Grid Logic for implementation.

Engagement duration varies by scope and complexity. A focused diagnostic assessment typically takes 2–4 weeks. A workflow automation implementation may take 6–12 weeks. Larger transformational projects can span several months. We will always provide a clear timeline with defined milestones before any work begins, and we respect your time and business continuity throughout every engagement.

We work with businesses of all sizes — from sole-trader operations to multi-branch enterprises and financial institutions. Operational efficiency is valuable at every scale. We tailor our approach and pricing to match the size and budget of the organization we're serving, ensuring that world-class operational thinking is accessible regardless of company size.

Grid Logic is fully equipped to deliver engagements remotely or on-site, depending on your preference and the nature of the work. Many of our clients across the Caribbean and internationally are served entirely through virtual collaboration. For local Jamaican clients, we can be on-site when the work requires it. We are flexible and focused on what's most effective for you.

Grid Logic has particular depth in financial services, investment management, professional services (legal, accounting, consulting), and small-to-medium enterprises across retail, distribution, and administrative services. That said, operational inefficiency is industry-agnostic — the frameworks we apply are adaptable to virtually any business context. If you're unsure whether your industry is a fit, reach out and we'll be transparent about our experience and capabilities.

We define success metrics at the beginning of every engagement — specific, measurable outcomes that matter to your business. This could include time saved per process, error rate reduction, reporting cycle acceleration, cost savings, or productivity gains. We track against these metrics throughout the engagement and provide a final impact report when the work concludes. We believe in holding ourselves accountable to the results we promised.

Every Grid Logic engagement concludes with full documentation, staff training, and a handover that ensures your team can sustain and build upon what we've implemented. We also offer post-engagement support retainers for clients who want continued advisory access. Many clients return to us for subsequent projects as their business evolves — and that's exactly the kind of long-term partnership we aim to build.
